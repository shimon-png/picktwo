Pickbot: answers tailored to you, checked by the crowd

Ask anything in plain chat (text, photos, links). Pickbot learns who you are, answers with Claude using your demographic and taste profile, then lets real people like you vote on it.

1Summary

Pickbot is a chat assistant inside crowdpick. You ask it a decision question the way you’d ask a friend (“Which of these two dresses for a winter wedding?” with two photos). Before answering, Pickbot makes sure it knows enough about you:

  1. Demographics first. If anything is missing, it asks one question at a time (sex, age range, relationship status, ZIP…) and explains that each answer makes its advice better.
  2. Then personality. If you haven’t voted on 30 battles yet, it offers a choice: a general answer now, or a tailored answer after a few more quick battles that complete your taste profile.
  3. Then the answer. Pickbot sends the question, your profile and what people like you picked on similar battles to Claude, and returns a clear recommendation with “why this fits you”.
  4. Then the crowd. It asks: “Want real people to weigh in too?” If yes, it turns your question into an anonymous battle, and brings you back when the votes are in.

Everywhere you vote, a profile bar shows how close you are to a complete profile (“12 more battles to unlock tailored answers”).

Why now

  • We already have the ingredients. About-you cards collect demographics one tap at a time; the taste profile scores six personality axes from votes; “people like you” results group votes by sex × age; AI tags describe every battle. Pickbot puts them to work for the person who gave them.
  • It gives voting a personal payoff. Today voting helps other people. With Pickbot, every vote makes your own answers better. That turns the 30-battle profile from a nice extra into a goal.
  • It feeds the crowd. Every “get human input” creates a fresh, real question for the Vote deck, and a reason for the asker to come back.
  • Unit economics. Our break-even per visitor is about $0.10 and mostly comes from sign-ups. Pickbot creates a strong reason to sign up (save your profile, get your answers back) and a natural paid tier.

2Goals, non-goals and success metrics

Goals

  • Give each person a better answer than a generic AI chat, because it knows them and what people like them chose.
  • Raise engagement: more battles answered per person, more return visits.
  • Complete more profiles (demographics + 30 battles).
  • Create a steady flow of real questions for the Vote deck.
  • Create a reason to sign up and a path to a paid tier.

Non-goals (v1)

  • Open-ended chat about anything. Pickbot is for decisions and opinions; it politely declines other uses.
  • Medical, legal or financial advice beyond general information (see §10).
  • A native app (web first; home-screen install and push later).
  • Sharing anyone’s answers or profile with other users.

4End-to-end flow

1AskText, up to 4 photos, or a link. Safety screen on photos and text.
2Demographics gateMissing core fields? Ask one at a time inside the chat. Skippable.
3Taste checkFewer than 30 battles? “General answer now, or tailored after N quick battles?”
4Quick battlesInline battles in the chat until the profile is complete (or the person stops).
5Claude answersQuestion + profile + crowd signals → recommendation, why it fits you, confidence.
6Feedback👍 / 👎, follow-up questions in the same thread.
7Human input?Yes → Pickbot drafts an anonymous battle; the asker reviews and posts it.
8Results returnWhen the battle closes (or hits enough votes), notify and summarize in the thread.

Purple = profile gates. Blue = AI. Pink = crowd. Every gate has an exit: a person can always skip to a general answer.

Rules that keep the flow pleasant

  • Never block the answer. Each gate offers “Skip, answer now”. Skipping marks the answer as general.
  • Ask only what matters. Extended demographics (kids, home, work) are asked only when the question is about them, as judged by a quick classifier on the question.
  • One question per message. Same tap style as about-you cards; answers shuffled; “never shown on your profile” line.
  • Remember everything. Answers given in Pickbot fill the same profile the deck uses, and vice versa. Nobody is asked twice.
  • Make the wait worth it. While doing quick battles, the bar shows “3 more and your answer is tailored”. The battles are picked to be relevant to the question’s theme when possible.

6Detailed requirements

Priority: P0 launch · P1 soon after · P2 later

6.1 Asking

PriRequirement
P0Plain-text questions up to 2,000 characters; up to 4 photos per message (JPEG/PNG/HEIC, resized before upload); links shown as cards. Follow-up messages in the same thread keep context.
P0Entry points: an Ask button in the header and on You; a “Not sure? Ask Pickbot” link after each battle result; the homepage “Learn your taste” section.
P0Every photo goes through the existing photo safety pipeline (screenPhotos: known-abuse hash match, explicit-content check) before Claude sees it. Text runs through the profanity filter and Claude’s own safety handling.
P0Scope guard: a fast classifier labels each question as decision / advice / off-topic / sensitive. Off-topic gets a short, friendly redirect; sensitive topics follow §10.
P1Voice input (browser speech-to-text) and paste-a-screenshot.

6.2 Demographics gate

PriRequirement
P0Before the first answer, check the four core fields (sex, age range, relationship, ZIP). Ask only the missing ones, one per message, in that order, using the about-you card style (shuffled tap answers, skip, “never shown on your profile”).
P0Each question explains the benefit in one line, e.g. “People in different life stages answer this very differently.”
P0Answers are saved through the existing answerAbout() path (same table, same carry-over from guest to account).
P1Extended fields (kids, home, work) are asked only when the question classifier marks them relevant (e.g. parenting, moving, career).
P0ZIP prefilled from the IP guess where available (“Near Austin? Confirm your ZIP”).

6.3 General or tailored

PriRequirement
P0If the person has answered fewer than 30 battles, offer the choice with the exact count remaining and a time estimate (~10 seconds per battle).
P0Tailored path: inline battles inside the chat, real open battles from the deck (votes count normally, points and all), preferring battles whose AI theme matches the question and that carry traits for the person’s weakest axes.
P0“Stop and answer now” always available; the answer is then marked with how complete the profile was.
P1Smart threshold: if the axes relevant to this question are already well measured, skip the extra battles even below 30.

6.4 The answer

PriRequirement
P0Structure: My pick (one clear choice, or “it depends on X” only when truly balanced) · Why it fits you (2–3 lines tied to the person’s traits and situation) · People like you (real crowd numbers on similar battles, when we have at least 20 votes) · Confidence and the one condition that would flip it.
P0Streams in word by word; first words within ~2 seconds.
P0Labelled “Tailored” or “General”. A general answer ends with “Want this tailored? N quick battles.”
P0👍 / 👎 on every answer, with an optional one-line reason.
P1Follow-ups (“what about shoes?”) in the same thread, reusing the profile and earlier photos.

6.5 Human input (the crowd)

PriRequirement
P0After every answer: “Want real people to weigh in?” If yes, Pickbot drafts a battle: a short title in the asker’s voice, two options, and the photos (if any).
P0Anonymized: the draft removes names, usernames, places finer than a city, workplaces, phone numbers, emails and other identifying details. Posted under a shared “Asked via Pickbot” identity, never the asker’s handle.
P0The asker always sees and can edit the draft before posting. Nothing is posted without an explicit tap.
P0Photos: the normal photo safety pipeline. Photos showing children’s faces are not allowed in battles (existing children’s safety rule); Pickbot explains and suggests a text-only version.
P0Default targeting: people like the asker (same sex, nearby age ranges), via the existing battle.tags.target. Default length 24 hours, editable.
P0Posting requires an account (one-tap sign-up card if they’re a guest), so we can bring them back.
P1Boost: spend ⚡ to get the battle shown to more people sooner (existing boost mechanics).

6.6 Results coming back

PriRequirement
P0When the battle closes, or reaches 50 votes if sooner, Pickbot posts a summary into the thread: split for everyone and for people like the asker, top reasons, and whether the crowd agreed with Pickbot.
P0Notification: Activity alert + email through the existing deliver() pipeline (quiet hours, caps, unsubscribe). Push when web push ships.
P0Ends with “Tell us what you picked” (closing the loop, existing ⚡ reward), which also teaches Pickbot whether its advice landed.

6.7 Profile bar

PriRequirement
P0A slim bar at the top of the Vote deck, Ask and You: “Profile 22 / 30 · 8 more to unlock tailored answers”. Counts battles answered toward tasteFullAt; shows a “+ 2 about you” hint while core demographics are missing.
P0Updates instantly after each vote; small celebration and the taste-profile reveal at 30; then it disappears.
P0Tapping it opens a short explainer: what a complete profile unlocks and that answers are never shown publicly.
P1For guests: “Sign up to keep your profile” at the free-vote limit (ties into the one-tap sign-up card).

7AI design (Claude)

Models

  • Answers: Claude Opus 5.5 (claude-opus-5-5), the current default Claude model. Adaptive thinking, effort medium (its default), streaming. Refusal fallbacks enabled.
  • Cheaper option: Claude Sonnet 5.5 (claude-sonnet-5-5) at half the price, if quality holds in testing. Our decision after the evaluation below.
  • Small jobs (question classifier, battle draft, anonymization check, picking relevant battles): Claude Haiku 5.5 (claude-haiku-5-5).

What Claude receives

  • The question, photos and earlier turns of the thread.
  • Profile as ranges only: sex, age range, relationship, region (state/metro, not ZIP), relevant extended fields.
  • Taste axes with scores and the type name.
  • Crowd signals: up to 10 similar battles (by AI theme and traits) with overall and like-you splits and top reasons.
  • Never: name, email, username, exact ZIP, other users’ data.

Prompt structure

  1. System prompt (fixed, cached): Pickbot’s role and voice (warm, direct, brief), the answer structure, how to use profile and crowd data, when to say “it depends”, safety rules for sensitive topics, never invent crowd numbers.
  2. Profile block (per person, cached per thread): demographics, taste axes, completeness.
  3. Crowd block (per question): similar battles and splits, retrieved by our server.
  4. Conversation: the question and follow-ups.

The answer comes back as structured JSON (pick, why, crowd note, confidence, flip condition) rendered by the app. Battle drafts use a second structured call (title, option A, option B, removed details) that the asker reviews.

  • After launch: 👍 rate and “crowd agreed with Pickbot” rate, tracked per model and prompt version.

10Privacy, safety and policy

Privacy

  • Questions and answers are private to the asker. Admins see them only for safety review of reported content.
  • Only ranges go to Claude (age range, region, never name/email/username/exact ZIP). Anthropic’s API terms: no training on our data.
  • Crowd battles are anonymous by design; the asker reviews every word.
  • Settings: delete any thread; account deletion deletes all threads; data export includes them.
  • Privacy policy update: what Pickbot stores, what is sent to the AI provider, how long we keep it (proposed: 12 months).

Safety

  • 18+ only (same as the product).
  • Sensitive topics (health, legal, money, relationships with abuse signals): general information only, suggest a professional; abuse or self-harm signals get crisis resources (988 in the US) and no crowd posting.
  • Photos: existing safety pipeline; no children’s faces in crowd battles.
  • No manipulation: Pickbot never pushes purchases or sponsored options in answers; sponsored content stays clearly labelled in the deck.
  • Abuse limits: rate limits, the existing ban/report system, flagged threads to the moderation queue.

11Experiments

  • Pickbot holdout (10%): stable group that doesn’t see Pickbot, to measure its real effect on votes, profile completion, sign-ups and D7 return.
  • Gate test: “general or tailored” choice vs always offering tailored first. Measure tailored completion and satisfaction.
  • Profile bar test: bar shown vs not shown in the deck (votes per visitor, profile completion).
  • Model test: Opus 5.5 vs Sonnet 5.5 for answers (satisfaction, crowd agreement, cost).

All four appear on the Brain page using the existing A/B framework (experiment_assignment, p-values).

14Risks and mitigations

RiskMitigation
The gates feel like homework and people leave before the answer.One tap per question, a skip on every step, a general answer always available, the gate test in §11.
Tailored answers aren’t noticeably better than general ones.Blind preference testing before launch; use real crowd numbers (our unique data), not just traits; tune the prompt on the evaluation set.
AI cost grows faster than revenue.Daily free cap, Haiku for small jobs, prompt caching, model test vs Sonnet 5.5, Plus tier.
Privacy leak through crowd battles.Automatic anonymization + mandatory human review by the asker + the existing photo pipeline.
Harmful or overconfident advice on serious topics.Topic classifier, sensitive-topic rules, crisis resources, no crowd posting for those.
Crowd battles from Pickbot flood the deck.Cap Pickbot battles per user per day; deck ranking (engagement and skip factors) already demotes weak battles.
Thin crowd data early on (few similar battles).Show crowd numbers only with 20+ votes; otherwise say so and lean on the profile.
