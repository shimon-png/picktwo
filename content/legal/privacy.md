# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

## What we collect
- Account details: email, name, username.
- Optional profile context: your country/region, interests, and the **age ranges** of your kids. We never ask for children's names, birthdays or photos.
- What you do: battles you post, votes, reasons, outcomes, and basic usage analytics.
- A random device identifier cookie so you can vote without an account.
- Where your visit came from (for example the website or ad campaign that linked you here), so we know which campaigns work.
- Optional "about you" answers you give in the app (for example woman/man, age range, relationship status, ZIP, kids, whether you rent or own, work). Skipping is always fine. They're never shown on your profile; we use them to show you how people like you voted and in group results.

## What we estimate (no questions asked)
To understand our audience as groups, we estimate a few things from what every browser sends:
- Your rough region (state and metro area) from your IP address, using our hosting provider's location lookup. We don't store your IP address for this.
- Your device type: iPhone or Android, the browser or app you opened us in, and the phone's price range (from screen size or model name).
- An estimated household income band (lower, middle or upper) from public Census figures for your ZIP area, adjusted by phone type.

These estimates are never shown to anyone, never used about you as an individual, and only appear in groups of at least 20 people.

## Advertising measurement
We use the X (Twitter) pixel to measure our ads on X. It tells X when someone visits crowdpick.ai and when a new account is created, so X can report how many sign-ups an ad brought. We don't send X your name, email, votes or anything you post. You can limit this with your browser's tracking protection or X's own ad settings.

We also use Vercel Web Analytics, our hosting provider's privacy-friendly visit counter. It counts page views without cookies and without identifying you.

## Pickbot (AI answers)
- When you ask Pickbot, we store your question, any photos you add and its answers in your account, private to you. We delete them after 12 months, and you can delete any question sooner, or all of them by deleting your account.
- To answer, we send your question, photos and **ranges only** to our AI provider, Anthropic (Claude): your sex, age range, relationship status, region (not your ZIP), the six taste traits from your votes, and how groups of people voted on similar battles. Never your name, email, username or exact location. Anthropic doesn't train its models on this data.
- Photos go through the same safety checks as battle photos before the AI sees them.
- If you choose to ask the crowd, Pickbot drafts an anonymous battle and you check every word before anything is posted. It's posted as "Asked via Pickbot", never under your name, and other people can't see it came from you.

## Payments and notifications
- If you get Pickbot Plus, Stripe handles the payment. We keep only a Stripe customer reference and when your Plus ends; we never see your card number.
- If you turn on notifications, your browser gives us a push address so we can tell you when results are in. Turn them off any time in your browser or phone settings.

## How we use it
- To run crowdpick.ai: show battles to relevant voters, calculate results, priority points and reputation.
- To send you notifications you've enabled.
- **Only if you opt in:** to create anonymised preference datasets that improve recommendations and AI models. Exports never include your name, email, username, or photos.

## Your choices
- Change your data-sharing consent any time in Settings.
- Download all your data from Settings (including your Pickbot questions and answers).
- Delete your account from Settings. Your votes remain only as anonymous counts so other people's results don't change.

## Children
crowdpick.ai is for adults 18+. Please don't upload photos where a child's face is visible. Reports of child-safety concerns are prioritised by our moderation team.
