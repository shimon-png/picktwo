# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

## What we collect
- Account details: email, name, username.
- Optional profile context: your country/region, interests, and the **age ranges** of your kids. We never ask for children's names, birthdays or photos.
- What you do: battles you post, votes, reasons, outcomes, and basic usage analytics.
- A random device identifier cookie so you can vote without an account.
- Where your visit came from (for example the website or ad campaign that linked you here), so we know which campaigns work.

## Advertising measurement
We use the X (Twitter) pixel to measure our ads on X. It tells X when someone visits crowdpick.ai and when a new account is created, so X can report how many sign-ups an ad brought. We don't send X your name, email, votes or anything you post. You can limit this with your browser's tracking protection or X's own ad settings.

We also use Vercel Web Analytics, our hosting provider's privacy-friendly visit counter. It counts page views without cookies and without identifying you.

## How we use it
- To run crowdpick.ai: show battles to relevant voters, calculate results, priority points and reputation.
- To send you notifications you've enabled.
- **Only if you opt in:** to create anonymised preference datasets that improve recommendations and AI models. Exports never include your name, email, username, or photos.

## Your choices
- Change your data-sharing consent any time in Settings.
- Download all your data from Settings.
- Delete your account from Settings. Your votes remain only as anonymous counts so other people's results don't change.

## Children
crowdpick.ai is for adults 18+. Please don't upload photos where a child's face is visible. Reports of child-safety concerns are prioritised by our moderation team.

## Contact
Questions about your data, or want it exported or deleted? [Contact the crowdpick.ai team](/contact).
