# Community guidelines

PickTwo works because people help each other make real decisions. Keep it kind, honest and safe.

## Do
- Post real decisions you're actually making.
- Vote honestly and add a reason when you can — it helps more than you think.
- Close the loop: tell people what you picked and how it went.

## Don't
- Post photos where a child's face is visible, or anything that identifies a child.
- Harass, insult or demean anyone.
- Post sexual, violent or hateful content.
- Spam, self-promote, or game priority points with fake votes.

## Reporting
Use the Report button on any battle, reason or profile. Child-safety reports are reviewed first and may hide content immediately.
